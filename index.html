GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is SaveReel actually free?

Yes — there's no subscription, no download cap, and no account required. The site is supported by on-page ads instead of user fees.

Does it remove the watermark?

Yes, for TikTok specifically — you get the clean source file rather than the version with the username overlay.

Do I need to install anything?

No. SaveReel runs entirely in your browser on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop.

Can I download private videos?

No. SaveReel only works with links to public videos, the same as opening them in a browser.

Who owns the videos I download?

The original creator does. SaveReel is a personal-use tool — please credit creators and get permission before reposting their work elsewhere.
